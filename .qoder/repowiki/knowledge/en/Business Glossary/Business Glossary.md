### Pujo Sahayak
- Definition：The product name of the mobile-first PWA being built — a digital companion for exploring Kolkata during Durga Puja, combining pandal discovery, local place discovery, an AI assistant (Maya), route planning, social meetups, safety tools, user-generated content, personalization, and monetization.
- Aliases：Pujo Sahayak

### Maya
- Definition：The in-app contextual AI assistant (not a generic chatbot). It answers questions about the current pandal/place, provides dataset-grounded information, supports chat/voice/camera scan flows, and is surfaced prominently from Categories and other screens rather than occupying one of the five bottom-nav tabs.
- Aliases：Ask Maya

### Pandal
- Definition：A temporary Durga Puja structure/decoration; in this app it is the primary Place subtype and the main discovery target during the Puja season, with detail pages covering theme/story, past editions, reviews, Top Clicks photos, similar places, nearby restaurants, and nearby meetups.

### Panchami
- Definition：Day 1 of the six-day Durga Puja calendar used as a tab label in the multi-day Puja Planner; the planner covers Panchami through Dashami.
- Aliases：Shashthi、Saptami、Ashtami、Navami、Dashami

### Meets
- Definition：The social feature/page where users discover or create activity-based meetups around shared interests (pandal hopping, photography, food, heritage, etc.) — explicitly not a dating app. Supports existing meetup cards and a Create Meetup flow with fields like title, date/time/location, description, interest chips, max participants, and public/restricted visibility.
- Aliases：Meetups

### Top Clicks
- Definition：A community-curated photo section on a Pandal Detail page showing visually popular photographs contributed by actual visitors; distinct from 'What others say' reviews.

### Beyond Puja
- Definition：The off-season experience / positioning: the app remains useful outside the Durga Puja season through restaurants, events, local activities, offers, and other discovery features — not limited to the festival.
